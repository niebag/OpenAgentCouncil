# Review recommendations before finalizing

The Chair produces a Draft Recommendation only after at least two Council Members have completed Positions, then submits it to one bounded Review Round. At least one participating Council Member must complete that review before the Chair can finalize a Recommendation. Without Quorum or a completed review, the result remains an Incomplete Deliberation. Research claims stay linked to concrete sources, and any Dissent remains attributed to its originating Council Member. This prevents the Chair from becoming an unchecked single point of bias.
